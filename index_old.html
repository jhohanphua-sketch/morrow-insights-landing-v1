Six components

What we measure — and why it matters.

Why these tests? As we age, physiological reserve tends to decline — the capacity your heart, lungs, and muscles have to handle stress and stay independent. MORROW Insights measures key markers of that reserve, so you can understand where you are today and where to focus next.
01
🫁

Aerobic Fitness

VO₂ max

How efficiently your heart, lungs, and muscles work under effort. A well-studied marker of cardiovascular fitness and long-term health, supported by peer-reviewed research.

02
🦴

Body Composition

DEXA Scan

Bone density, muscle mass, and visceral fat distribution. Provides detailed body composition data that goes beyond what BMI or scales can show.

03
💪

Physical Resilience

Strength & Power

Functional capacity through validated strength and power tests. Physical resilience is closely linked to healthy ageing and long-term independence.

04
🌬

Respiratory Health

Spirometry

How well your lungs move air and support endurance and daily energy. An important marker that most routine screenings overlook.

05
🩸

Metabolic & Cardiovascular

Blood Biomarkers

Metabolic health, lipids, glucose, inflammation, and nutritional status. May reveal early metabolic shifts worth monitoring over time.

06
🧠

Lifestyle & Resilience

Psychological Well-being

Mental and emotional health factors that influence daily functioning. Well-being and resilience are core components of long-term healthspan.

Common questions

Frequently asked.

How does MORROW Insights differ from a typical health screening? +
Most health screenings focus on detecting disease markers. MORROW Insights measures your physiological reserve — fitness, body composition, metabolic health — and provides an educational Group Discussion to help you understand your results and what to do next. It's designed as a starting point for ongoing tracking, not a one-off check.
Who is MORROW Insights for? +
MORROW Insights is for health-conscious adults who want a clear, evidence-informed picture of their current healthspan — and practical guidance on where to focus next. It suits those who are generally well but want to go beyond reassurance and take a more proactive approach to their long-term health.
How does MORROW Insights complement my existing healthcare? +
MORROW Insights is designed to complement your existing care, not replace it. Your GP provides important clinical oversight and medical management. MORROW adds a deeper functional picture — fitness, body composition, metabolic patterns — and education to help you make more informed lifestyle decisions alongside your regular healthcare.
What happens during the Group Discussion? +
The Group Discussion is a 90-minute educational session with 6–10 participants, led by a clinician. It walks through key findings, common patterns, and how to interpret your screening results. It is an educational session and does not replace a 1:1 consultation. For a personalised review, a separate 1:1 consultation is available.
Is this a one-time report or is there follow-up? +
MORROW Insights is designed for tracking and reassessment over time. After your initial screening and Group Discussion, your clinical team will guide you on when to re-test and what to monitor. Optional follow-up and tracking services are available.
How long does the whole process take? +
Allow approximately 2 hours 40 minutes for your testing visit. The Group Discussion is a separate 90-minute session. You will be advised on scheduling during the booking process.
Do I need to fast before the assessment? +
Yes, fasting is required for accurate blood biomarker results. We'll provide full preparation instructions after you book, including fasting duration and any other requirements.
